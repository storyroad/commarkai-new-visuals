# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between:

**COMMARKAI** ("Provider"), with a place of business in Ottawa, ON, Canada,

and

**[CLIENT LEGAL NAME]** ("Client"), with a place of business at [CLIENT ADDRESS].

## 1. Scope of Services
Provider will perform services described in one or more Statements of Work ("SOW")
executed under this Agreement. Each SOW forms part of this Agreement by reference.
In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and a SOW, the SOW governs for
that specific engagement only.

## 2. Fees and Payment
Fees, payment schedule, and invoicing terms are specified per SOW. Unless a SOW
states otherwise, invoices are due within [15/30] days of issue. Late payments may
accrue interest at [1.5%] per month on the overdue balance, to the extent permitted
by Ontario law.

## 3. Term and Termination
This Agreement remains in effect until terminated by either party with [30] days'
written notice. Either party may terminate immediately for material breach not
cured within [15] days of written notice. Termination of this Agreement terminates
all open SOWs unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## 4. Intellectual Property
Upon full payment for a given SOW, Client owns the specific deliverables created
for that SOW. Provider retains ownership of all pre-existing tools, frameworks,
templates, and general know-how used to deliver the work, and may reuse them for
other clients.

## 5. Confidentiality
Each party agrees to keep the other's confidential information private and use it
only to perform under this Agreement, for [2] years following disclosure.

## 6. Warranties and Disclaimer
Provider will perform services in a professional and workmanlike manner. Except as
expressly stated, services are provided "as is" without other warranties, express
or implied.

## 7. Limitation of Liability
Neither party's total liability under this Agreement will exceed the fees paid by
Client under the applicable SOW in the [12] months preceding the claim. Neither
party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

## 8. Independent Contractor Relationship
Provider is an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, or partner of
Client. Nothing in this Agreement creates a joint venture or partnership.

## 9. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal
laws of Canada applicable therein.

## 10. General
This Agreement, together with all executed SOWs, is the entire agreement between
the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes prior discussions.
Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
