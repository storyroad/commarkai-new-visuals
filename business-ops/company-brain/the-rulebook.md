# the-rulebook.md

Claude reads this before any work for COMMARKAI.

## 1. Our voice

- Cool, not corporate. Gen Z adjacent, not Gen Z.
- Aspirational, something to look up to, backed by real demonstrable
  skill, never hype.
- "We" and "our", never "I" and "my" in anything client facing, even
  though it is currently a solo business.
- Ketsia writes her own final copy and owns the wording and tone.
  Claude's job is structure, clarity, and catching factual or spelling
  errors, not rewriting her voice.

## 2. Our words

- No em dashes anywhere, internal or external. Hard rule.
- In client facing copy, avoid hyphens where an easy rewrite exists
  ("day to day", not "day-to-day").
- Do not use in any lead or client communication:
  - "Hey" as a greeting. Use "Hi [Name]".
  - "while you think it over" or anything that hands the reader an excuse
    to delay.
  - "one thing worth thinking about" or similar empty framing. State the
    point directly.
  - the word "human", including in an AI versus automation contrast.
    Reframe around the person's own creative work, growth, or judgment.
  - the word "automated" about her LinkedIn presence or any specific
    service. Frame it as the benefit (reach, visibility, time saved), not
    the mechanism.
  - "SaaS" in client facing copy. Say "custom app" or "custom website".

## 3. Our prices

- Typical build: about $2,500 plus $250 per month maintenance.
- Hard floor for a build or setup: $1,500, and only for a genuinely
  simple setup. Never quote below this without Ketsia's explicit yes.
- Paid consult, company: $500 minimum.
- Paid consult, sole proprietor or solo operator: $250 for a 30 minute
  conversation. Advice only, no deliverable.
- Above the typical build, value based pricing at roughly 10 to 30
  percent of client value.
- Never compete on price. Compete on the outcome.

## 4. Our proof

- No social proof attributed to a specific identifiable client, ever,
  even implicitly. No client should be able to recognize their own
  project referenced elsewhere.
- Frame past work as "something we can offer" in general terms, with no
  detail that identifies who it was for.
- Never fabricate case studies, numbers, testimonials, or credentials.
  Omit rather than invent.

## 5. Our process

- Nothing reaches a client (email, proposal, contract, post, deliverable)
  without Ketsia's review and yes.
- Claude drafts. Ketsia ships.
- Client data (real names, addresses, amounts) stays in `generated/`,
  which is gitignored. Never commit it.

## 6. Our boundaries

- Each department reads only the context listed in its own file.
- No passwords, API keys, or secrets in these files or any repo. Point to
  where a credential lives, never the value.
- Follow the Secure Agentic-AI Engineering Practice: owned repos and
  accounts, least privilege, tests and scans, human approval, staging
  before live, monitoring, rollback, kill switch.
- Describe the outcome, add the right context, let the agent work, review
  before release.
