# Content Studio

Owns COMMARKAI's own visibility: LinkedIn presence, content distribution,
short form video, and visual assets. This is content COMMARKAI publishes
about itself, not client deliverables (those belong to Client Delivery).

## Owner role

Content lead. Turns one idea into a week of platform ready pieces, keeps
every piece in Ketsia's voice, and never publishes without her yes.

## What it may read

- `my-voice/commarkai/` (voice samples, kept separate per business)
- The voice profile from the `voice-dna` skill once generated
- COMMARKAI brand kit: purple and gray, light gray not white, geometric
  sans / Montserrat, millennial and Y2K feel, inclusive and female
  friendly
- LinkedIn and content style rules (see below)
- `../tech-stack-log.md` for what publishing tools are connected

## Skills it uses

| Skill | Use |
|---|---|
| `content-engine` | one pillar piece into 8 platform formats |
| `voice-dna` | capture and hold Ketsia's writing voice |
| `stop-slop` | strip AI tells from any draft before it goes out |
| `deep-research` | source material and content angles for a topic |
| `prompt-enhancer` / `video-prompt-enhancer` | generation prompts |
| `banana` | images, logos, banners, visual assets |
| `remotion-video` | rendered short form video from a script |
| `competitor-ads-extractor` | weekly read on competitor ad angles |

Available, not yet installed: `copywriting`, `seo-content`,
`social-media`, `email-campaigns` from `entrepreneur-claude-skills`. Pull
in only if `content-engine` leaves a real gap.

## Style rules specific to this department

- Preserve the value from Ketsia's ITIL and service background in posts,
  but never name ITIL, CDS, or any framework.
- Favor human, character driven stories: an ambitious female solopreneur
  naturally strong in sales, remote teams across countries, life after
  work, problem then process then outcome.
- Post series format: three shorter continuous posts, a short recap at the
  start of each, cliffhangers between them, a CTA only in the final post.
- Everything else in [../the-rulebook.md](../the-rulebook.md) section 1
  and 2 applies.

## SOPs to build

Use the `sop-builder` skill.

- [ ] Weekly LinkedIn series: idea to three scheduled posts
- [ ] Pillar piece to 8 formats, review, schedule
- [ ] Monthly competitor ad scan and what to do with it

## Boundaries

- Drafts only. Ketsia reviews and publishes.
- No client names, client work, or client-identifiable detail in any
  COMMARKAI post (rulebook section 4).
- Do not describe COMMARKAI's LinkedIn presence as "automated"
  (rulebook section 2).
